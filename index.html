Breaking · 2027 line-ups

Lawson and Lindblad stay at Racing Bulls for 2027

Racing Bulls have confirmed an unchanged line-up for 2027: Liam Lawson and Arvid Lindblad have both extended their contracts. The announcement on Thursday ends weeks of speculation that Lawson could lose his seat to Red Bull junior Nikola Tsolov, second in the Formula 2 standings.

Tsolov instead becomes the reserve driver for both Red Bull and Racing Bulls next season. That leaves the 2027 grid almost complete: the one seat still unannounced is at Haas, where Oliver Bearman and the team have both said they want to continue.

Lawson's season

89 points and ninth in the championship after Brazil, including seventh from a pit-lane start in Mexico.

Lindblad's rookie year

38 points and 11th in the championship in his first Formula 1 season.

The last seat

Haas's second car is the only 2027 drive still to be confirmed.

Nov 10 · Calendar

The season will finish in Qatar and Abu Dhabi as planned

Formula 1 has confirmed that the final two rounds go ahead in Qatar on November 29 and Abu Dhabi on December 6, and has stood Imola down as its back-up venue. All 11 teams backed the decision. F1 says it will keep monitoring the situation in the region.

Oct 13 · African Grand Prix

F1 is going back to Africa: Rwanda signs a 10-year deal for an African Grand Prix

Formula 1 has agreed a 10-year deal with Rwanda to stage the African Grand Prix, the sport's first race on the continent since the 1993 South African Grand Prix at Kyalami. It was announced in Kigali on Tuesday, with President Paul Kagame and F1 chief executive Stefano Domenicali.

There is no date yet. The race is aimed at the early 2030s, 2030 at the earliest, because the circuit still has to be built: a new permanent road course at Bugesera, about 40 km south of Kigali, next to the country's new international airport, which is due to open in the second half of 2028.

Carsten Tilke and former F1 driver Alex Wurz have designed a layout of around 6 km with 17 corners, run anti-clockwise between two hills that form a natural grandstand and alongside a lake. Wurz called it a roller coaster: the lap rises and falls 197 metres in total, almost twice Spa-Francorchamps' 102, and it includes a kilometre-long back straight into a hairpin. The design is finished and now moves into engineering.

33 years away

F1's last race in Africa was the 1993 South African Grand Prix at Kyalami. Rwanda formally bid to bring it back in December 2024.

Built for the hills

A natural road course rather than a street circuit, with 197 metres of elevation change a lap and a long back straight that sets up an overtaking spot into the hairpin.

Still to come

The first race date, the final track length (reports range from 5.3 to about 6 km) and the construction milestones will be announced later, F1 says.

No circuit drawing yet: the track is not built and no official layout data has been published, so we will add one when it is.

Recap · Round 21 · Las Vegas

Verstappen survives a Strip thriller as the top four finish within 2.6 seconds

Max Verstappen won a race full of lead changes in Las Vegas, 0.639 seconds ahead of Lando Norris, his fourth win of the season. Oscar Piastri completed a McLaren double podium from 17th on the grid, his first podium since Miami, and Kimi Antonelli was fourth: the first four were covered by 2.574 seconds at the flag.

Antonelli had set the fastest time in qualifying, but a five-place grid penalty for power-unit components put Verstappen on pole.

LAS VEGAS GP · LAS VEGAS STRIP CIRCUIT · NOV 21, 2026

A collision between Sergio Pérez and Rafael Câmara at Turn 14 brought out the Safety Car around lap 19, and almost everyone pitted under it. From the restart George Russell and Antonelli took turns attacking Verstappen, and the lead changed hands again and again. Russell's challenge ended when he locked up and hit the barrier at Turn 5 in the closing laps; he recovered to ninth. On the final lap Antonelli lost second to Norris and Piastri into Turn 1.

Pierre Gasly and Franco Colapinto took fifth and sixth for Alpine. Lewis Hamilton was seventh and Charles Leclerc tenth after the two Ferraris collided again, with Leclerc given a ten-second penalty. Carlos Sainz was eighth from 20th on the grid. Arvid Lindblad spun out behind the Safety Car, Pérez retired after the collision and takes a five-place grid penalty into Qatar, and Alex Albon did not start with a brake problem. Liam Lawson set the fastest lap.

2.574 seconds

The gap from first to fourth. The first eight finished within 13 seconds.

Second place

Verstappen 304, Hamilton 267, Russell and Norris 264. Leclerc (250) can no longer finish second.

Constructors' third

McLaren's 33 points cut Red Bull's lead in the fight for third to nine: 440 to 431.

Drive of the day · McLaren

Piastri goes from 17th to the podium

Starting 17th after a ten-place grid penalty, Oscar Piastri came through a chaotic race to finish third, 1.529 seconds behind the winner and his first podium since the Miami Grand Prix.

With Norris second, McLaren scored 33 points, the most of any team in Las Vegas.

Qatar Grand Prix — Race Preview

Round 22 · Lusail International Circuit · Nov 27–29

With both titles decided, Qatar is about second place. Max Verstappen leads the fight on 304, ahead of Lewis Hamilton (267), George Russell and Lando Norris (264 each), with two Grands Prix and 50 points left. If Verstappen leaves Lusail more than 25 points clear of all three, second is his.

The Qatar Grand Prix is a standard night weekend: practice on Friday, qualifying on Saturday at 9pm and the 57-lap race on Sunday 29 November at 7pm local time. Pirelli brings its three hardest compounds, the C1, C2 and C3, and has limited every set of tyres to 27 laps, so nobody can finish the race with fewer than two stops.

Tyre compounds

The Lusail International Circuit runs 5.419 km clockwise over 16 corners, most of them fast and flowing, with a main straight of more than a kilometre. Built for MotoGP, it is hard on tyres: the long, high-speed corners load them for lap after lap, which is why Pirelli limits stint length here.

Fight for second

Verstappen 304, Hamilton 267, Russell 264, Norris 264. Leclerc (250) is out of the running.

Form guide

Verstappen has won the last three Qatar Grands Prix (2023, 2024 and 2025); Hamilton won the first, in 2021.

The 27-lap rule

Safety Car laps count towards the limit. Last year's 25-lap rule made every driver in the top 10 stop exactly twice.
