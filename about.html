Why this site exists

Most F1 coverage online is either a copy-paste of the press release or a hot take with no follow-through. This journal aims to sit in between: clear-eyed recaps, breaking news explained with actual context, and the occasional deep dive into strategy or sim racing data.

No sponsorships, no paywall, no team affiliation — just a fan who watches every session and writes it up properly.
