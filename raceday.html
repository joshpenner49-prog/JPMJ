How the facts are checked

Every figure in the programme carries a label, so you always know where it came from. Each edition lists its sources and the date its facts were last checked. All times are local to the circuit.

Verified
From an official or primary source: the FIA, Formula 1, Pirelli or the published results.
Measured
Computed by us from timing data or the official track outline, with the method stated.
Estimate
A sourced estimate, with its assumptions stated. Never presented as a measurement.
Analysis
Our editorial view: a reading of the facts, labelled as opinion.
