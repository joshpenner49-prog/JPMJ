Reaction challenge

Lights Out

Five red lights. Then darkness. That instant is when a real Grand Prix starts — wait for all five to go dark, then hit the button as fast as you can.

Driver reaction time

F1 drivers average around 200 milliseconds to react to lights out at the start of a race — close to the physiological limit for human reaction.

Jump start rule

Sensors detect any wheel movement before the lights officially go out. React early in a real race and it's an automatic penalty — no such penalty here, just bragging rights.

Bonus fact: pit stops

The fastest recorded F1 pit stop is 1.82 seconds — Red Bull, 2019 Brazilian Grand Prix. That's wheel nuts off, tyres changed, nuts back on, car released.
